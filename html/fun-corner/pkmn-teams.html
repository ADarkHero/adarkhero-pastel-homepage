My furry kitten, named after my girlfriend. With their ability to change types they can STAB their opponents to victory.

Schneeball 7

Lost Maiden

Mega Froslass took my mega slot in this playthrought. I already really liked it in Pokemon Champions - but the champions meta was always kinda bad for Froslass. In this game, they had "Intimidate" as an ability. A kinda weird choice, but always a good ability.

Secretariat

Stamina Guy

My horse with the "Stamina" ability. It basically got unkillable by physical attackers. It would set up double team and heal itself with rest.
