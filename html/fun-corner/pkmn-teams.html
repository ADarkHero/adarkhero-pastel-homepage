PKMN-Teams

Are you interested, in what teams I used to beat Pokemon (or other monster tamer) games? Look no further!

Pokemon Lazarus

Quaxelquak: My trustworthy rain setter with hypnosis-capabilities to catch wild Pokemon easily.
Nyuum: My furry kitten, named after my girlfriend. With their ability to change types they can STAB their opponents to victory.
Powerbank: I won't need a phone charger anymore with this mon! In later battles it was a bit too slow for my taste tho.
Schneeball 7: Mega Froslass took my mega slot in this playthrought. I already really liked it in Pokemon Champions - but the champions meta was always kinda bad for Froslass. In this game, they had "Intimidate" as an ability. A kinda weird choice, but always a good ability.
Secretariat: My horse with the "Stamina" ability. It basically got unkillable by physical attackers. It would set up double team and heal itself with rest.
Amaterasu: They gave ninetales the fairy type. I gave ninetales the "Drought" ability. It dealt tons of damage with fire and fairy attacks!

Super Mariomon

Ice King: I always loved Paper Mario and this one was a great boss of that game. Kinda weird, that it wasn't a legendary in Mariomon.
Poison Pokey: Setting poison-spikes is always fun. :3
King Bob-Omb: My trustworthy starter and a bruiser with good tanky capabilities and nice damage.
Yoob: The pseudo-legendary. Do I have to say more? Tanky as heck, solid damage, just a bit slow.
Plungelo: Cataquack is one of my favorite Mario characters, so I had to use it's evolution. Also special fighting types are always funny.
Muncher: THE iconic "Super Mario World" enemy. I decided to give it a leech seed stall set. :3
