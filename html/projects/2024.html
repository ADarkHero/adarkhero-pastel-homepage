🟢 FolderWatcher

A little tool I made for work actually. It checks, if directories contain any files and sends Windows alerts, when they do. It can also check, when files were last changed.
We have ERROR folders at work. If there are files in that folder, something went wrong and somebody should check it. After the error is fixed, the file must be moved manually to a archive-folder.

FolderWatcher on GitHub

🔴 Schätze des Widerstands (working title)

It was a board/card game hybrid, I wanted to create. In the end, the concepts didn't quite work out, so I dropped it. Maybe I'll revive it in the future?
The games goal is to collect treasure points. To collect them you must convert cards to treasures. The main gimmick was, that players created the board themselfs. You could activate cards to use their effects instantly or put them face down on to the board. If a player moved onto that card it got activated. Many cards were traps, with negative effect, if you landed on them.

Read the rules (German only)
Play on Steam via Tabletop Simulator

🔴 RPG Game (working title)

I actually wrote a decent amount of lore for this one. But I never did more with it. I wasn't sure if I wanted to do a classic rpg (maker) game or a visual novel.
The main protagonist was unhappy with his life. One day they meets a mysterious cat near a closed of forest area. They follow the cat into the forrest where a mythical world awaits them. In this world, everyone has multiple lives - the cat (now human) has only one of their nine lives left. What happened to her? Why is she "recruiting" people from our world?
