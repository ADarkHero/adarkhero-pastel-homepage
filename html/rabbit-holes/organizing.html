Rating Movies and TV Shows

I still rate all movies and shows I watch on a scale from 1-10. That way I can always see my favorites very easily. Content I adore gets a 7 or higher. 8s are my favorites. I rarely give 9s or 10s but when I do, I'm absolutely in love with it. If I give a rating of 5 or lower, I wouldn't watch it again. 6 is a weird rating - the show/movie had some nice and rememberable parts but overall didn't really stick with me.
