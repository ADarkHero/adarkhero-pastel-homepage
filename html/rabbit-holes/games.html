Rabbit holes I fell into

League of Legends (Classic)

"Should I commit sin again?", I asked my girlfriend one faithful day. League Classic just released, many of the old folks rejoined the game, so I wanted to taste the cake too.
Unpopular opinion: It's still fun! I had many good games and League still tickles a special itch.
Also I played with some old friends, some of which I already played League over 10 years ago, which is fun.

Pokemon Champions

The release of Champions was my entry into the competitve Pokemon scene. I never owned/played Sword/Shield/Scarlet/Violet, but a competitve focussed game was right up my ally. Relatively fast, I focussed on my trusty Pellipper rain team. But I also had good success with meta-slop or Froslass/Scovillain teams.

Pokerogue

Why do Pokemon fans make better games than Gamefreak themselfs? I mean, I think ZA was an okay game, but it had so many issues.
As an enjoyer of roguelikes in general Pokerogue caught my interest quickly. It focusses battles and randomly generated elements. Every run feels fresh. Many Pokemon were rebalanced to actually do something.

Pokemon Kaizo Ironmon

It's one of my favorite things to watch on Twitch. Kaizo Ironmon is an absurdly hard challenge, that usually takes about 1000 tries to complete. Some take only a minute, some hours. What makes it so hard? Everythings random. Your starter, the Pokemon you face, the items... Your opponents have their level increased by 1.5. You're not allowed to defeat wild Pokemon to level. You're not allowed to buy healing items... There are many more rules.

It's kinda like watching a kid beat up their face against a wall. Fun and fascinating.

Cassete Beasts

I ranted over mothern modern Pokemon games earlier... Cassete Beasts is a really good indie monster tamer game. Something that I wished, modern Pokemon games would be. It got heart, awesome monster designs, great soundtrack and many new fresh ideas. It doesn't use the classic double/half damage for effective attacks, it uses status effects. Water heals plants, fire makes plastic poisonous.

Persona 5

Probably the best JRPG ever made, ranked 8th on my top-100 games list. I like the core gameplay loop that levelling up your friendship makes you stronger in battles. All characters are nicely written, the story is great, the game looks fantastic, Futaba is the best waifu.
I played throught this game 3 times and spent like 150 hours with it. And it had only 2 boss fights and one dungeon-mechanic I didn't vibe with. I love this game.

Marvel Snap

I also loved this game. I played like 200 hours before the monitization finally drove me off. The biggest issue is aquiring new cards. In the beginning, you get tons cards to start with. Then they started introducing new cards. With paywalls and randomness. It was litereally impossible to get all the cards in the game - which is fine, if you can trade them for playable ones (like master duel or even hearthstone did) - and getting a competitve deck or playing with the "new toys" sometimes felt impossible. I probably should have dropped it earlier, but the core gameplay is still tons of fun.

Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel

I always liked Yu-Gi-Oh! growing up but took a break in the 5Ds/Synchro era. I came back to the game during the covid era, when it got bigger on YouTube. I started watching Cimoooooooo (with 8 o's, may you rest in peace, I loved your videos), but actually buying cards and creating competitve decks was always too expensive. When Master Duel dropped, I was back baby. I have like over 800 hours in the game by now and still enjoy it, although I play less than during the release period.
