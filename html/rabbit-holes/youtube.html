Falsch aber lustig

It's a German comedy show on YouTube. I was always kinda into German comedy, although most of it was kinda bad. In that show, three lesser known comedians get on stage and have to answer questions. The interesting part: They don't have to find the correct answer, they have to find the funniest. There are so many sympathic and actually funny people in this show. The host is also really likeable. I'm always happy, when a new episode releases.

Linus Tech Tips

It took me some time to get, why LTT is this successfull. I actually can't remember how I got into it but some day I startet watching every video of that channel that remotely interested me. I like Linus, he's a nice host. The topics are interesting almost every time.

VShojo

After getting into VTubers with Hololive, getting into VShojo was a clear next step. It's sad, that it bursted into flames in the end. Many ex VShojo members are my favorites to this day (Ironmouse, Nyanners) and they had so many other nice talents on there.

Hololive

I got into Hololive with it's first EN-generation. I have to say, I didn't watch future generations that much but the first one really caught my eye. VTubers were kinda new back then - at least outside of Japan. I liked all talents of the first EN-generation, but Calli was my favorite by far. I loved her first few songs, her newer works sound too "commercial" to me - for the lack of a better description.

OfflineTV

The first two "seasons" of OfflineTV were really special to me. They consisted of so many people I like: DisguisedToast, LilyPichu and Pokimane. MarkZ was cool, Scarra is a nice dude, Yvonne is sweet. I also really liked Fed and sleightlymusical back in the day. It's funny, that those two people got cancelled at the end of season 2. lul I was also a huge fan of "Just Friends", friends of OfflineTV consisting BoxBox, Fuslie and iGumdrop. They split up sadly.
In the end I didn't really like the new talents OfflineTV adopted and I slowly stopped watching.
