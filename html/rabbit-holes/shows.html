shows

Adventure Time

It's 2026, Adventure Time is still one of the most creative series out there. Fionna & Cake was a great addition to the series, atleast as good as the original show if not better. Distant Lands was also great but the weakest link of the series. I finally got to watch the whole thing this year and had a great time with it.

One Piece

Probably my favorite anime by now. I watched every single episode. It's fascinating how this show, after over 1100 episodes, still delivers an interesting story and never gets boring.
It took me a while to get into One Piece actually. I wasn't a huge fan of Alabasta and Skypia and dropped it in the past. By Water 7 I was hooked. I watched the entire rest of the series in like half a year and got every available episode on dvd/blu-ray. I also rewatched many parts via One Pace, a cut down alternative that removes any unneccessary/filler content.

The Owl House

I have to say, the beginning of this show was nice, but I really got hooked with the LuzXAmity prom episode. I fell in love with the show at that point and it's one of my all time favorites to this day. Every character is so nice and the story itself is also catching. Also: Queer representation is always nice.

Naruto

Naruto was probably my first real anime or at least the first one I fell in love with. Maybe unpopular opinion but the original Naruto anime (without the fillers) is still one of the best anime experiences you can have. The Lee vs. Gaara fight is still an all time favorite of mine. Jiraiya and Kakashi are still characters I really adore. Shippuden was great. I haven't completed Boruto yet but I still think it's a fine show.
