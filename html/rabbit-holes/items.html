Headphones

I sadly don't have unlimited money and I listen to speakers or IEMs most of the time. So I "just" own one good pair of headphones - the old reliable Sennheiser HD600 (hurr durr six hundros). Listening to music is so much fun with them. A really great piece of technology at an okay price range.

IEMs (in ear monitors)

I had many different ones over the years because they tend to break or wear off after heavy use. Chi-Fi (chinese hi-fi) models are usually my go to. The're priced competitivly and sound really good. My favorites to this day are the Moondrop Blessing 2. The're a bit big but with the right ear tips, they fit me well.
I also was never a fan of true wireless earbuds. Everything I tested just sounded bad. Most € 20,- chifi IEMs outperform € 200,- true wireless earbuds. That's kinda sad.
