Ado

In my opinion the best singer, that ever praised this planet with existence.
For a while, my favorite thing was looking at reactions to her live cover of "Unravel". It's so fun to see 40-50 year olds, who have no connection to the scene, be fascinated by her.

FAKE TYPE.

It's hard talking about why exactly you like music. Music is more of a feeling, I guess? FAKE TYPE. has a really unique genre, a mix of electroswing and rap. I think, it really sounds awesome. Every song also sounds unique. They recently got a major label deal. I didn't really enjoy their newer songs that much.
